# Brandmaster: Features and Roadmap

## The purpose of Brandmaster

Brandmaster helps the company answer a simple but important question: **Are we recognizing and describing this brand correctly?**

Brands can arrive with different spellings, aliases, identifiers, or incomplete information. People often have to investigate whether a name is already known, which record it belongs to, or whether it should be treated as a new brand. Brandmaster brings those decisions into one place, helps people make them consistently, and preserves approved knowledge for future work.

The long-term vision is for Brandmaster to become both a **brand quality workspace** for people and a **trusted brand checking service** that other company applications can use.

## Current features

The current app focuses on helping people resolve brand mapping work and prepare reliable results.

### 1. Bring brand work into one queue

Teams can import lists of unmapped brands, add names directly, and organize work that needs attention. This replaces scattered lists with a focused workflow.

### 2. Find likely matches

Brandmaster checks submitted names against the reference information available to it, including prior decisions, aliases, and brand tables. It can use these sources to recommend a likely match or identify records that need a closer look.

### 3. Review and correct decisions

People can review suggestions, change names or identifiers, and record notes or evidence. Uncertain decisions remain visible for human judgment rather than being silently accepted.

### 4. Prepare a clean result

Once the work is reviewed, Brandmaster can create a standardized mapping file for the existing upload process. This gives teams a clear handoff from investigation to operational use.

### 5. Support shared team work

The app includes ways to coordinate team work, track pending and completed items, keep review history, and see aggregate progress. Available collaboration depends on how the app is deployed and connected.

### 6. Work in different settings

Brandmaster can run locally or offline and can also be deployed as a web app. This gives teams a way to work with the tool in different environments while the shared data and access model continue to mature.

**In brief:** today, Brandmaster helps people find, review, and prepare brand mapping decisions. The future capabilities below describe the larger product opportunity; they are not all available in the current app.

## Scalable features: where Brandmaster can grow

### 1. Maintain the company’s brand records

Connect Brandmaster to both the established Brand table and the Unknown Brand table. This would let teams compare known brands with new or unresolved entries and work from the company’s shared information.

### 2. Improve and standardize brand data

Give teams a guided way to correct names, add or reconcile aliases, standardize records, identify duplicates, and merge entries that represent the same brand. Approved changes could be sent back to the company’s main Brand table, with a clear record of what changed and who approved it.

### 3. Validate brands wherever they are used

Let other company applications check a brand with Brandmaster as information is entered or processed. The answer could say whether the brand is recognized, which approved record it maps to, whether the information looks valid, or whether a person should review it.

### 4. Combine company knowledge, smart suggestions, and human judgment

Use company data and agreed quality rules to check identifiers, names, aliases, status, and possible duplicates. AI could help identify likely matches, summarize evidence, and flag unusual cases. Reviewers would confirm changes when the answer is uncertain or consequential.

### 5. Make brand knowledge reusable

Keep approved decisions available for future work so teams and connected applications do not have to solve the same brand question repeatedly. Over time, this shared knowledge can make mappings more consistent across the company.

### 6. Show where quality needs attention

Provide simple views of unresolved brands, duplicate candidates, conflicting information, aging records, review workload, and progress. This helps teams focus effort where it will have the most value.

## Product roadmap

The roadmap below is a suggested sequence. Each stage builds on the previous one and should be shaped by the teams who will use Brandmaster.

### Stage 1: Make today’s workflow dependable

**Focus:** Make brand review easier to use and easier to trust.

- Clarify the review steps and decision outcomes.
- Make it easy to see why a match was suggested.
- Improve visibility into unresolved work and team progress.
- Agree on the key fields and rules used to identify a brand.

**Benefit:** Teams can complete current mapping work with greater consistency and less confusion.

### Stage 2: Work from shared brand information

**Focus:** Connect the workflow to the company’s established and unknown brand records.

- Bring the Brand table and Unknown Brand table into the review process.
- Compare records to find likely matches, gaps, and duplicate candidates.
- Keep a clear history of proposals, reviews, and approved decisions.

**Benefit:** Teams make decisions using a more complete and consistent view of company brand data.

### Stage 3: Improve the brand records

**Focus:** Move from preparing mapping files to improving the source information.

- Support controlled edits, standardization, alias updates, and merges.
- Let people review proposed changes before they take effect.
- Return approved updates to the Brand table with traceable history.
- Provide a way to correct or reverse a mistaken change.

**Benefit:** Approved cleanup improves the shared brand information itself, so the same issues are less likely to recur.

### Stage 4: Let other applications check brands

**Focus:** Make trusted brand decisions available across the company.

- Allow approved applications to ask whether a brand is recognized and valid.
- Return the matching company record and a clear result, such as confirmed, needs review, or not found.
- Support both individual checks and larger batches.
- Route uncertain cases to the Brandmaster review process.

**Benefit:** Applications can use a consistent answer instead of maintaining separate brand lists and rules.

### Stage 5: Expand coverage and insight

**Focus:** Grow based on proven use and business need.

- Add more approved data sources and useful integrations.
- Improve detection of duplicates, conflicts, and outdated information.
- Expand reporting on data quality and time saved.
- Extend coverage to additional teams, regions, or brand types as needed.

**Benefit:** Brandmaster becomes a shared capability that can grow with the company.

## How the pieces fit together

| Need | Brandmaster’s role |
|---|---|
| A new or unfamiliar brand appears | Find possible matches and gather it for review |
| Two records may describe the same brand | Show the possible relationship for a person to confirm |
| Brand information is inconsistent | Recommend corrections and standardization |
| A change could affect the company’s records | Require review and keep a history of the approved change |
| Another application needs to check a brand | Return the shared, approved brand answer |

## What success looks like

Brandmaster should be judged by whether it makes everyday work better. Useful measures include:

- Less time spent researching and resolving each brand.
- Fewer repeat investigations of brands already reviewed.
- Fewer duplicate or conflicting brand records.
- More consistent brand information across teams and applications.
- Faster review of new or uncertain brands.
- More applications using the same approved brand information.

The company should first measure current effort and data quality, then set realistic improvement goals as each roadmap stage is introduced.

## The opportunity

Brandmaster starts by helping people resolve brand mappings. It can grow into the place where the company **checks, improves, and trusts its brand information**—with people and AI working together, approved changes improving the source records, and other applications able to use the same reliable answer.

**One shared way to recognize brands. Better information for every team that depends on it.**
